Built for Nigeria

The new tax rules mean more invoices, done right. Emily keeps up.

Since 1 January 2026 the Nigeria Tax Act 2025 is in force, and the Nigeria Revenue Service (NRS, formerly FIRS) is rolling out e-invoicing through the Merchant Buyer Solution (MBS). Every invoice now has to be correct and on time.

7.5%

Standard VAT rate, unchanged under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025. Emily also handles zero-rated and exempt lines.

₦

Invoices in naira, with the client's Tax ID, as your software expects. Other currencies when your software allows them.

MBS

Invoices are created in your software. If it is connected to an NRS-accredited provider, the IRN and QR code are added there, as usual.

24/7

Requests that arrive at night or on weekends are ready the next morning, not the next week.

  1. Live since 2025Large taxpayers

    Turnover of ₦5 billion or more. Full compliance expected from 31 July 2026.

  2. Go-live 1 July 2026Medium taxpayers

    Turnover of ₦1 to 5 billion. Enforcement from January to March 2027.

  3. Go-live 1 July 2027Emerging taxpayers

    Turnover below ₦1 billion. Most of your smaller clients fall here.

Sources: NRS public notices of 17 February and 20 July 2026; Nigeria Tax Act 2025. Emily is not an NRS-accredited System Integrator or Access Point Provider: she works inside your invoicing software, which connects to the MBS through its own accredited provider.

Frequently asked questions

The firm decides everything. Emily only issues what she can confirm.

Is it compliant?

The invoice is issued by your own invoicing software, exactly like one typed by hand. If your software reports to the NRS, that keeps working as it does today.

What about data?

EU servers, a data processing agreement, and the NDPA and GDPR respected. The AI does not train on your data.

What if she gets it wrong?

When in doubt, she asks and does not issue. She flags VAT mistakes and never issues the same invoice twice.

Who is in control?

She only accepts requests from emails the firm has authorised. To stop her, block her user in one click.

Which software?

Live today with TOConline, in Portugal. For Nigeria, we build the connection to QuickBooks, Zoho Books, Sage, Xero or Odoo with the first firms, through each official API.

How long does it take?

Once the connection to your software exists, about a week. Your firm only chooses the email for requests; we do the rest.

Do clients talk to a machine?

They talk to the firm's assistant, who signs with the firm's name and says she is AI. Anything that needs a decision always goes to the accountant.

Power or network cuts?

Emily runs in the cloud, not in your office. Requests sent during an outage are handled as soon as they arrive.

Who is behind Emily?

4Digital, a Portuguese company working with accounting firms. Emily has been in production since November 2026.

Privacy policy · last updated 25 November 2026

1. Who we are

The controller of the data collected on this site is 4Digital, a company registered in Portugal (“4Digital”). For any question about personal data, write to miguel.barros@4digital.pt.

2. Data we collect on this site

When you ask for a demo or a test, we collect your name, firm, email, phone (optional), invoicing software, approximate number of invoices and your message. We use no advertising or tracking cookies. We use it only to reply and arrange the demo or test, and keep it for up to 12 months after the last contact if no contract follows.

3. Data Emily processes for firms

When an accounting firm uses Emily, the firm is the data controller for its clients' data and 4Digital acts as data processor, under a data processing agreement signed with each firm, in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and the EU General Data Protection Regulation. In that case:

  • We process only the data needed to issue invoices, collect documents and answer requests, on the firm's instructions.
  • Data is processed in a private Google Cloud service, on servers in the European Union. Access keys are kept in Google Secret Manager.
  • Requests are analysed with an artificial intelligence model from a provider that does not use the data to train its models. Transfers outside the country of origin are made only with contractual safeguards.
  • Issued invoices stay in the firm's invoicing software.
  • The firm's clients who wish to exercise their rights should contact their firm; we help the firm respond.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell data. We use providers that process data on our behalf, under contract: form delivery by email (Web3Forms), Google Cloud infrastructure (EU), Google email and the artificial intelligence model provider.

5. Your rights

You may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, object to processing, and withdraw consent, by writing to miguel.barros@4digital.pt. We reply within one month.

6. Security

We use encrypted connections, least-privilege access, keys kept outside the code and an audit log of Emily's actions. If there is a data breach, we alert the affected firms without delay.
